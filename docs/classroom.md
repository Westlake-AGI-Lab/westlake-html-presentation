# Classroom And Practice

Students join a room anonymously; teachers authenticate separately. Teacher-only questions, feedback summaries, likes, opt-in danmaku and follow-teacher navigation retain their existing behavior. Raw private AI chats are not automatically sent to teachers.

Teachers extract concepts from slide text/notes or edit them manually. They review source quotations, explanation/application/counterexample tasks, criteria, two hints and worked answers before publishing. A valid quotation proves textual grounding, not pedagogical correctness.

Students answer in their own words. Criterion statuses are correct, missing, incorrect or uncertain; aggregate states are understood, partial, misunderstood or uncertain. Hint one requires an attempt; hint two requires another. Explanations unlock after criteria are met or after two attempts and both hints. These states are evidence observations, not calibrated mastery.

Try-first mode gates assistance controls, while Explore remains an explicit alternative. Existing slides, diagrams, notes and previous explanations remain visible; this is not exam security. Contextual checkpoints enter the matching question. Local concept history is saved only when opted in, and teacher summaries require separate consent. Summary counts are not yet automatically used to draft lecture improvements.

The learning archive stores conversations/images in the browser when enabled. Concept practice records are separate. Explanatory slides are local drafts until explicitly exported/shared. See the [user guide](user-guide.md) for controls and the [research status](research.md) for missing validation.
